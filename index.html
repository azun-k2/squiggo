Draw along, step by step

Follow the dotted line, tap the rings, colour in the shapes. The buddy says each step aloud and celebrates with one to three stars. Scoring never says "wrong": a wobbly line still counts.

  • Easy
  • Medium

  • Cartoon pals
  • Storybook friends
  • Yummy treats
🎨

Magic Art Tools

One-tap bucket fill that hugs the lines, a rainbow brush that flows through every hue, and a neon glow pen that shines softly. Plus classic crayon, marker, paintbrush, glitter, and stickers with real tactile drawing sounds.

🎬

Watch It Draw (Timelapse Replay)

Relive every brushstroke in a delightful animated timelapse replay with your personal pencil leading the way, ending with cheerful cheers and celebratory confetti!

🐻

Make your own buddy

Choose a built-in buddy, draw one, or turn a photo into a buddy. Squiggo lifts the subject off its background right on your device. Add googly eyes, a mouth, eyebrows, hair and glasses, choose how it moves, and give it a name it will say out loud.

🖼️

My Wall & Collectible Frames

Finished pictures go up on your personal gallery wall. Dress your masterpieces in handcrafted frames: Warm Wood, Rainbow Ribbon, Starry Galaxy, or Candy Cane swirl.

🔔

Tactile sounds & kind voice

Real waxy crayon friction, felt-tip glides, bubbly paint plops, and gentle cheerful encouragement in 34 languages. No internet required, ever.

🔒

Private by design

No ads, no accounts, no tracking and no network access at all. Pictures stay on your device unless a grown-up shares one. Squiggo never asks for your photo library: the system picker shares only the one picture you choose. The full privacy policy says the same in more detail.

💻

Made for the Mac

Draw with the mouse or trackpad, pinch to zoom, use the keyboard shortcuts, and drag a picture in from the Finder. Also on iPhone and iPad.

Requires an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, or a Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Android tablets are coming.

Questions grown-ups ask

What ages is it for?

Children who like to draw, with a grown-up nearby to start.

Does it need the internet?

No. Squiggo has no network access.

Is there a subscription?

No. Buy once and it is yours on every device.

Where do the pictures go?

They stay on your device. A grown-up can share one on purpose.

Does the photo buddy upload my photo?

No. The subject is lifted off the background on your device.
